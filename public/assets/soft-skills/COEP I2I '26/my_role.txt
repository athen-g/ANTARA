As the Database Manager for Team Unimark during the Ignited Innovators of India (I2I) competition at COEP Technological University, I was responsible for designing and managing the project's database architecture, ensuring efficient, secure, and scalable data handling for our centralized smart academic management system. Throughout the competition, I contributed to the technical development and refinement of the platform, working closely with my teammates to transform the idea into a functional solution. I also participated in project discussions, presentations, and the final exhibition, helping showcase Unimark to judges, mentors, and industry professionals. Our collective efforts led to Unimark securing Second Prize in the Education Category after progressing through multiple competitive phases from over 690 participating teams.
