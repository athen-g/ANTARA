🚀✨ From Phase 1 to the Podium ✨🚀

We’re incredibly proud to share that our project unimark secured 🥈 Second Prize in the Education Category at Ignited Innovators of India (I2I), hosted at the COEP's Bhau Institute of Innovation, Entrepreneurship and Leadership, COEP Technological University.

This journey has been nothing short of transformative.

Starting from Phase 1 with 690+ participating teams, we progressed to being shortlisted among the Top 150 teams for Phase 3, where we refined our idea under the guidance of mentors from Eaton.
A special thanks to our mentor Dhanashri Shirkhedkar for her valuable insights and continuous support.

Advancing further, we were selected among the Top 40 teams for Phase 4, taking us to the final stage at COEP.

📍 Day 1 – Bhau Institute, COEP
We attended the inauguration and pitched unimark - a centralized smart academic management system - to a panel of judges. Presenting on such a prestigious platform was an incredible experience.

📍 Day 2 – Exhibition & Valedictory
We showcased our project during the project exhibition, interacting with guests, mentors, and fellow innovators. The journey concluded at the Valedictory Ceremony, where we were awarded Second Prize in the Education Category.

This wouldn’t have been possible without my amazing team:
Mandar Navarkar, Allen Joseph and Usmeet Sonawane — the effort, resilience, and belief we shared made this possible.

We are also deeply grateful to Dr. Nuzhat Shaikh, Head of the Computer Engineering Department, for being our guide and supporting us throughout this journey and to our college Modern Education Society's College of Engineering, Pune

A heartfelt thank you to our Ambassador Shubhra Ahirrao and Head Manaswi Bele for their constant coordination and support and to Aditya Kasod (Secretary – Ignited Innovators of India) and Vikas Kamble (Publicity Officer – I2I), along with the entire I2I team, for creating such a powerful platform for student innovators.

From an idea to the podium — and this is just the beginning. 🚀
