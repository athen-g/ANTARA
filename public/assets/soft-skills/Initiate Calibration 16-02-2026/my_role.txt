As the Design Head of HYPERSPACE XR SIG, I played a key role in organizing our inaugural event, *Initiate Calibration*. I designed the event's promotional materials, including posters and branding assets, and co-designed the event presentation to ensure that the content was both visually engaging and informative for attendees. During the event, I also managed the photography, capturing important moments and documenting the experience for our community. In the latter half of the event, I took charge of the VR session, guiding students through their first immersive XR experience and allowing them to enjoy a VR roller coaster ride, making the event both interactive and memorable.
