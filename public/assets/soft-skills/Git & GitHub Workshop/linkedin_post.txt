🚀✨ Successfully Conducted Git & GitHub Workshop ✨🚀

Yesterday, all the SIGs of Modern Education Society's College of Engineering, Pune came together for a collaborative Git & GitHub Workshop, bringing students under one platform to strengthen their fundamentals in version control and collaborative development.

This initiative was a joint effort by:
🔹 MESWCOE SIG HYPERSPACE (XR)
🔹 App/Web Development (SIG) 
🔹 Cybersecurity SIG
🔹 SIG - AIML & DS

As a member of Hyperspace, I was excited to take part in this session and had the opportunity to conduct the workshop alongside Mandar Navarkar and Gayatri Dombali.

We covered essential Git concepts, practical GitHub workflows, branching strategies, collaboration techniques, and real-world project practices, ensuring participants not only understood the theory but also implemented it hands-on.

Workshops like these truly highlight the power of cross-SIG collaboration and peer-led learning.

A heartfelt thank you to Dr. Nuzhat Shaikh, Head of the Computer Engineering Department and Faculty Coordinator of Hyperspace SIG, for her continuous support and encouragement.
Special thanks to Dr. Sharmila Wagh, Faculty Coordinator of all SIGs, for fostering collaboration and creating such impactful learning opportunities.

Looking forward to more collaborative sessions ahead! 💻🔥
