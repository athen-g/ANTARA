As the Design Head of HYPERSPACE XR SIG, I contributed to the successful organization of *Render Creation*, a two-day Unity Workshop conducted in the MESWCOE XR Lab. I was responsible for designing the event's visual identity and promotional materials, including posters, digital creatives, and other branding assets that helped promote the workshop. In addition to my design responsibilities, I assisted in conducting the workshop, guiding participants through the fundamentals of Unity, game development concepts, and the creation of an interactive archery game. Through hands-on sessions and practical demonstrations, I helped create an engaging learning environment that enabled students to gain their first experience in 3D game development and XR-related technologies.
