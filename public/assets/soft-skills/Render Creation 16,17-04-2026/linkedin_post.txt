🎮 Two Days. One XR Lab. Countless "aha" moments.

Last Thursday and Friday (April 16–17), I had the privilege of being part of something genuinely exciting Render Creation, a 2-day Unity Workshop hosted by MESWCOE SIG HYPERSPACE right here in our Modern Education Society's College of Engineering, Pune XR Lab.

From 12 to 4 PM each day, we guided students through the world of 3D game development from setting up scenes and working with physics, to building a fully interactive Archery Game in Unity Engine from the ground up.
What struck me most wasn't just the technical growth I watched happen in real time it was seeing students go from "I've never opened Unity" to holding a certificate of completion within 8 hours.

As the Design Head at Hyperspace, this event meant a lot to me personally. Beyond the visual identity and materials we put together, getting to witness students genuinely light up when their game logic finally clicked that's the whole point.

Proud of every team that made this possible the Tech Team for architecting the experience, the Event Team for seamless execution, and our incredible Head of Department Dr. Nuzhat Shaikh Ma'am and faculty coordinator Prof. Sagar Shinde Sir for their continued support.

Here's to building more, learning more, and rendering the future. 🚀
